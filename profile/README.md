# Lost Skies – A High-Flying Co-Op Survival Adventure

Lost Skies is a breathtaking open-world co-op survival game set among floating islands in the sky. Developed by Bossa Studios, creators of Surgeon Simulator and I Am Bread, this game combines exploration, crafting, and aerial traversal in a stunning, dynamic world.

![capsule_616x353 (1)](https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/d9ea8cbe-9e71-4712-a6ee-1605daf559b0)

In Lost Skies, players must band together to build airships, discover ancient ruins, and survive hostile environments—all while soaring through the clouds. Whether solo or with up to 6 players in co-op, your journey is shaped by creativity, teamwork, and discovery.

Key Features
Build Your Own Airship
Design, craft, and customize your skyship to travel between floating islands.

Explore a Dynamic World
Procedurally generated floating islands filled with ancient technology, secrets, and danger.

Survive Together
Fight hostile creatures, gather resources, and craft essential tools with friends.

Co-op Gameplay (1–6 Players)
Share the experience with friends as you repair, fly, and survive together.

Stunning Skyborne Environments
Rich biomes, changing weather, and immersive visuals make every journey memorable.
